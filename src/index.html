✉ Email notifications

Get an email when a backup starts or finishes, or when the app was closed and a scheduled backup was missed.

What to fill in
  • Send notifications to — the inbox that receives the emails.
  • Sender email + password — the account the app sends from.
  • SMTP host + port — Gmail is prefilled (smtp.gmail.com, 465). For a company mailbox, ask your mail admin for the server and port.
What NOT to put here
  • Your normal Gmail password — Gmail needs an App Password (Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification → App passwords).
  • Your backup job password or the master password — those belong to encryption, not to email.
  • The app only ever emails the address in “Send notifications to” — nothing else.
